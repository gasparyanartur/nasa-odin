## Google Earth check (2026-11-03)

Claim tested: "The advanced professional subscription of Google Earth has a map of Mars."

Verdict: PARTLY TRUE, and misleading. Mars is in free Google Earth Pro desktop. Google's tier page never lists it as a paid feature.

- Tiers: Standard $0, Professional $75/user/month, Professional Advanced $150/user/month. The feature lists do not mention Mars, planets or space. Advanced adds Earth layers such as EV charging, parcel zoning and traffic signals. Source: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/earth/earth-plans
- Mars in Earth Pro desktop: confirmed, from a very old release note. Source: https://support.google.com/earth/answer/40901?hl=en
- Google Maps has a planet view at google.com/maps/space/. The help page says "planets" and does not name Mars. Source: https://support.google.com/maps/answer/91511?hl=en
- Google Earth on the web: no primary page says it has Mars.
- Mars data inside it (missions, resolution, age): no primary source found. Secondary sources mention CTX, HiRISE and THEMIS layers; they were not opened.
- API, export, or paid features working on Mars: unconfirmed, no primary page found.
- Earth Engine: no Mars dataset turned up in search. The catalog itself was not checked.
- Licence: Google Earth "may not be embedded online or in apps". A handful of attributed static images is allowed for non-commercial use. No 3D reconstruction from its output. Commercial or promotional use is barred, so check before any startup-style pitch. Source: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/

Result: treat Google Earth as a viewer for looking around, and do not plan the product on it.
