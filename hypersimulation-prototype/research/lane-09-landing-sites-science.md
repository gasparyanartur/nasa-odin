Richest locations:
- Gale: most complete ground truth (rover data), top-3 workshop vote, and 2025-26 boxwork results. NASA says "boxwork patterns haven't been spotted anywhere else on the mountain" and "groundwater table had to be pretty high" (Tina Seeger). Caveat: Curiosity was due to leave the boxwork in March 2026, so those are rover results from a place a human crew could not yet plan around.
- Deuteronilus Mensae and Arcadia: the strongest ice-access case (SWIM abstract names both), which is the main resource argument for an EZ. Both lie at 43-49N, above the roughly +/-50 degree latitude bound NASA used in 2015 (per a secondary search snippet, UNCONFIRMED); Arcadia's top-of-ice depth is disputed.
- Melas Chasma and Jezero: highest participant votes, but I found no ice or resource evidence for them in pages I opened.
- For a layered-map hackathon: Gale and Jezero have the densest free data; the gazetteer WKT polygons can draw zone outlines.
